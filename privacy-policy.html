Information we collect

We collect information in the following ways:

Information you provide. When you contact us through our website form or by email, we collect the details you choose to share. That typically includes your name, work email address, company name, and project details or message content.

Security and anti-abuse data. Our contact form uses Google reCAPTCHA to help prevent spam and abuse. Google may collect device and usage data as part of that service under its own privacy terms.

Technical information. Like most websites, our servers and hosting infrastructure may automatically receive limited technical data when you visit, such as device type, referring URL, pages viewed, and timestamps. We use this information to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the site.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information (such as health, biometric, or government ID data) through this website. Please do not include that kind of information in form submissions.

We use personal information to:

  • Protect against spam, fraud, and misuse

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Legal bases (where GDPR / UK GDPR apply)

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files (and similar technologies such as local storage) that a website may place on your device. We use them only as needed to run the site and protect it — for example, to load pages securely, support session functionality, and power spam protection on our contact form via Google reCAPTCHA.

Some cookies or similar technologies may be set by third parties that provide those services (such as Google for reCAPTCHA). Those providers process information under their own privacy terms.

You can view, manage, and delete cookies through your browser settings, or block them from some or all sites. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of this site from working as intended.
