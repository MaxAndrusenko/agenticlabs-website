Service Cloud Custom Development

Cutting case resolution time 40% with a custom triage engine.

A support organization was losing time to manual case triage and misrouted work. We designed and built a custom Service Cloud triage engine that classifies and routes cases automatically — with a senior engineer owning every rule.

The problem

Manual triage was the bottleneck.

Every inbound case was triaged by hand. Agents read, categorized, and reassigned work before anyone could start solving it — and under load, cases landed in the wrong queue.

Misrouted cases bounced between teams, first-response times slipped, and priority issues waited behind routine ones. The support organization needed routing it could trust without adding headcount to the triage step.

Our approach

Classification and routing, engineered — not guessed.

A senior architect designed the triage model against the support team's real categories and priority rules before any code was generated. AI accelerated the build; a senior developer owned every classification path and routing decision.

  • Apex classification. Senior-built Apex classes score and categorize each inbound case against the org's real taxonomy, not a generic keyword match.
  • Flow routing rules. Declarative Flow routing sends each case to the correct tier and queue, keeping business rules visible and maintainable for in-house admins.
  • Omni-Channel setup. Omni-Channel distributes routed cases to the right agents by skill and capacity, so priority work reaches the right person first.
  • Test coverage. Apex tests and staged deployment protect the routing logic, with edge cases and priority overrides covered before launch.
  • Human-reviewed. Every rule was reviewed by a senior engineer, and escalations stay under human control rather than being silently auto-closed.
The result

Faster resolution, fewer escalations.

With triage automated, cases reach the right team on the first pass. First-response times dropped, resolution sped up, and the volume of misrouted work that had to be escalated or reassigned fell sharply.

The support organization now handles a higher case volume without adding triage headcount — and because the routing lives in reviewed Apex and Flow the in-house team can read, it stays maintainable as the categories evolve.
