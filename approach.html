Why we exist

Built for the gap in the middle of the market.

Most teams evaluating a Salesforce partner face a bad choice. On one side sit agencies that win on price by staffing junior, offshore benches and letting clients absorb the cost of learning on the job. On the other sit vendors overselling AI — promising that a model can architect and ship production software on its own, then handing over code no senior engineer would sign off on.

We started Agentic Labs because neither model respects the work. Salesforce orgs are load-bearing systems. The failure modes are expensive, and they show up months later — in a data model that can't scale, an integration that silently drops records, or an automation that behaves fine in a demo and breaks under real volume.

Our bet is simple. Senior judgment paired with agentic tooling is the right model — not senior judgment alone, and not tooling alone. The AI removes the grunt work so experienced engineers spend their time on design, edge cases, and the calls that actually decide whether a build holds up. That is where expertise earns its keep, and it is the one thing AI can't do for you.

How we work

Three principles behind every engagement.

Senior-only

Senior-only delivery

There is no junior bench and no offshore rotation absorbing hours on your budget. Every engagement is led and executed by architects and senior engineers who have shipped in real production orgs and lived with the consequences.

That means the person scoping your work is the person doing it. You are not paying senior rates to supervise juniors learning on the job — you are paying for engineers who already know where the platform bites.

Leverage

AI as leverage, not a shortcut

We use agentic tooling for the mechanical parts of development — boilerplate, scaffolding, first-draft tests, and documentation. It compresses the hours that used to go to typing so our engineers can spend them on judgment instead.

What AI does not do is decide. Every line it drafts is read, understood, and owned by a senior human before it goes anywhere near your org. The AI does the typing; the judgment stays human.

Rigor

Enterprise-grade rigor

Source control, code review, meaningful test coverage, and staged release management are not optional add-ons — they are how we work by default, on every project regardless of size.

AI-accelerated code is held to exactly the same bar as hand-written code, because your org can't tell the difference and can't afford downtime. If it wouldn't pass review written by hand, it doesn't ship because a model wrote it faster.

Process

From scope to shipped, without surprises.

01

02

Architect

03
