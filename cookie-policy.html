What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site's owners. Similar technologies — such as local storage, pixels, and tags — may be used for comparable purposes, and references to "cookies" in this policy include those technologies where relevant.

Types of cookies we use

The categories below describe the kinds of cookies this site may use. Specific cookies, providers, and retention periods are to be confirmed with the client.

  • Strictly necessary

    Required for the site to function — for example, to load pages securely and remember actions taken during a session. These cannot be switched off in our systems.

  • Preferences

    Remember choices you make — such as language or region — to provide a more personalized experience.

  • Analytics

    Help us understand how visitors use the site, in aggregate, so we can measure and improve performance.

  • Marketing

    Used to deliver and measure relevant messaging, and may be set by us or by third-party partners.

Managing your preferences

How to control cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you view, manage, and delete cookies through their settings, and to block cookies from some or all sites. The exact steps vary by browser; consult your browser's help documentation for instructions.
