Case study · Experience Cloud

A dealer and customer portal for an automotive retailer.

We built one connected portal on Experience Cloud and Service Cloud — customers book service and track their vehicles, while dealers manage inventory and leads from a single workspace.

Start a similar project ← All case studies
Industry

Automotive retail

Region

Multi-dealer

Platform

Salesforce Experience Cloud · Service Cloud

The challenge

Customers had no self-service way to book service or check on their vehicles, so everything ran through phone calls. Dealers, meanwhile, juggled inventory and leads across disconnected tools with no single place to work.

What we built

  • A customer portal for service booking and vehicle tracking.
  • A dealer workspace for managing inventory and leads in one place.
  • Service Cloud case handling behind the scenes so requests are tracked and resolved.
  • Automated notifications that keep customers updated on service status.

The outcome

Customers now self-serve bookings and vehicle information instead of calling in, dealers work leads and inventory from a single connected workspace, and the retailer gained clearer visibility across service and sales.
