How we approached it

01 Challenge

Manual intake couldn't keep up — and the data was sensitive.

Patient intake was manual and didn't scale with a growing waitlist. The information involved was sensitive and needed careful, privacy-conscious handling, and the startup's small team couldn't keep pace with onboarding by hand.

02 What we built

A secure portal that lets patients onboard themselves.

We built a self-service onboarding portal with privacy designed in from the first field — so a small team could scale intake cleanly.

  • ✓Secure self-registration — patients enrol and submit structured intake forms themselves.
  • ✓Privacy-conscious data model — access controls so sensitive information is seen only by the right people.
  • ✓Automated routing — new intakes go to the right team with no manual triage.
  • ✓Built to scale — a foundation that grows with enrolment as the startup grows.
03 Outcome

Intake scales; the team oversees instead of operating.

The startup scaled patient intake without adding headcount, sensitive data is handled with appropriate safeguards, and onboarding moved from a manual bottleneck to a self-service flow the small team can oversee rather than operate by hand.
