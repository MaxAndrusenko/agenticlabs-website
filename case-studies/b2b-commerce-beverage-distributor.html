Case study · B2B Commerce

Self-serve wholesale ordering for a Canadian beverage distributor.

We replaced phone, email, and fax ordering with a compliant Salesforce B2B Commerce storefront — so trade buyers reorder in minutes and the sales team is off manual data entry.

Start a similar project ← All case studies
Industry

Regulated alcohol & beverage distribution

Region

Canada

Platform

Salesforce B2B Commerce · Integration

The challenge

Trade buyers placed orders by phone, email, and fax. Every order was re-keyed by a sales rep, pricing and stock availability were opaque to buyers, and the business had to enforce the rules that come with selling a regulated product — licensing, provincial restrictions, and quantity controls — on every transaction.

What we built

  • A compliant B2B Commerce storefront with account-specific catalogs, contract pricing, and negotiated terms.
  • Reorder-from-history and saved lists so recurring wholesale orders take minutes.
  • Regulatory guardrails built into the buying flow to enforce licensing and jurisdiction rules on restricted products.
  • Integration to the order-management and back-office systems so online orders flow straight through without re-keying.

The outcome

Buyers now self-serve their reorders around the clock instead of waiting on a rep, order-entry errors dropped as manual re-keying disappeared, and the sales team was freed to focus on relationships and new accounts rather than transcribing orders. Pricing and availability are consistent and transparent for every buyer.
