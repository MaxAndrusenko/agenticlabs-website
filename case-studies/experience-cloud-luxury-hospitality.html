Case study · Experience Cloud

A concierge guest portal for a luxury hospitality group.

We unified fragmented guest data behind a single branded Experience Cloud portal — so guests self-serve bookings and requests, and staff work from one guest view that powers repeat-visit personalization.

Industry

Luxury hospitality

Region

International

Platform

Salesforce Experience Cloud · Integration

The challenge

Guest data was scattered across properties and booking systems, so no one had a complete picture of a guest's history or preferences. There was no unified, on-brand self-service channel, which made the kind of personalization a luxury brand depends on slow and manual.

What we built

  • A branded Experience Cloud portal where guests manage bookings and submit concierge requests.
  • A unified guest profile consolidating history and preferences across properties.
  • Integrations to booking and property-management systems so the portal reflects live reservations.
  • A staff console that surfaces the same single guest view to front-of-house teams.

The outcome

Guests now have one branded place to manage their stay, staff act on a complete guest profile instead of piecing it together, and the group can personalize for repeat guests at scale — with far less manual coordination between properties.
