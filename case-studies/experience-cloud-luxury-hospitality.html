How we approached it

01 Challenge

Guest data was scattered across properties.

Guest data was scattered across properties and booking systems, so no one had a complete picture of a guest's history or preferences. There was no unified, on-brand self-service channel, which made the kind of personalization a luxury brand depends on slow and manual.

02 What we built

One branded portal, one unified guest profile.

We unified guest data behind a single branded portal — for guests to self-serve, and for staff to work from one complete view.

  • ✓Branded Experience Cloud portal — guests manage bookings and submit concierge requests in your brand.
  • ✓Unified guest profile — history and preferences consolidated across every property.
  • ✓Booking & PMS integrations — the portal reflects live reservations, not stale copies.
  • ✓Staff console — front-of-house works from the same single guest view.
03 Outcome

Guests self-serve; staff personalize at scale.

Guests now have one branded place to manage their stay, staff act on a complete guest profile instead of piecing it together, and the group can personalize for repeat guests at scale — with far less manual coordination between properties.
