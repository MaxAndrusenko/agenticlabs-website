How we approached it

01 Challenge

Finance and sales kept re-keying between two systems.

Finance lived in Xero and sales lived in Salesforce, with people manually re-keying invoices and payments between them. The two systems drifted out of sync, numbers didn't match, and reconciliation was slow and error-prone.

02 What we built

A resilient two-way sync that keeps both systems honest.

We connected Salesforce and Xero with a two-way sync engineered to surface problems rather than fail silently.

  • ✓Two-way sync — invoices, payments, and contacts flow both directions automatically.
  • ✓Robust field mapping & error handling — mismatches surface instead of failing silently.
  • ✓Retry & idempotency logic — transient failures never double-post or drop records.
  • ✓Monitoring & logging — a clear view of every sync and any exceptions.
03 Outcome

One reconciled set of numbers, kept in step.

Finance and sales now work from the same reconciled data, manual re-keying is gone, the month-end close is faster, and reconciliation errors dropped because the two systems stay in step automatically.
