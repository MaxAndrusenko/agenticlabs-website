Case study · Integration

A two-way Salesforce–Xero sync for a fintech company.

We connected Salesforce and Xero so invoices and payments flow automatically in both directions — giving finance and sales one reconciled set of numbers, with no manual re-keying.

The challenge

Finance lived in Xero and sales lived in Salesforce, with people manually re-keying invoices and payments between them. The two systems drifted out of sync, numbers didn't match, and reconciliation was slow and error-prone.

What we built

  • A two-way integration syncing invoices, payments, and contacts between Salesforce and Xero.
  • Robust field mapping and error handling so mismatches surface instead of failing silently.
  • Retry and idempotency logic so transient failures never double-post or drop records.
  • Monitoring and logging that gives the team a clear view of every sync and any exceptions.

The outcome

Finance and sales now work from the same reconciled data, manual re-keying is gone, the month-end close is faster, and reconciliation errors dropped because the two systems stay in step automatically.
